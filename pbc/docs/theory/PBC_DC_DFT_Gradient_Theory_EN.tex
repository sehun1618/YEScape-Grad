\begin{abstract}
This document derives the energy and fixed-cell nuclear analytic gradient of
density-corrected density functional theory evaluated with semilocal target
functionals on restricted closed-shell periodic Hartree--Fock densities.  The
central complication is that the target energy is not stationary with respect
to the HF orbitals.  Consequently, the gradient requires the HF CPHF orbital
Hessian and an adjoint Z-vector equation.  The presentation develops the
notation and implementation conventions for FFTDF, Gaussian density fitting
(GDF), range-separated density fitting (RSDF), GTH pseudopotentials, Gamma and
sampled k-points, and the PBE and SCAN/r2SCAN target functionals in one
consistent framework.  It also covers a target multigrid integrator selected
independently of source GDF/RSDF, matrix-free MINRES Z-vectors, and
bounded-memory response algebra.  Stress, cell derivatives, multigrid PP, and
open-shell response are outside the active scope of this edition.
\end{abstract}
